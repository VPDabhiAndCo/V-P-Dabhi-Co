Allied Services

Complete business registration and compliance solutions for startups and growing enterprises.

Overview

Companies in India are governed by the Companies Act, 2013, which sets out statutory requirements for transparency, accountability and compliance. We help companies meet these requirements.

Our services cover company incorporation, ongoing filings, audits and governance advice, so you can stay compliant and focus on running your business.

Company incorporation

We handle the complete incorporation process: name approval, preparing and filing the Memorandum of Association (MoA) and Articles of Association (AoA), and obtaining the Certificate of Incorporation from the Registrar of Companies (RoC). After incorporation, we also apply for the company's PAN and TAN.

ROC filings and records

We file annual returns and financial statements with the RoC, along with event-based filings such as changes in capital structure and appointment of directors. We also maintain and update the statutory registers and records.

Audit, Directors' Report and AGM

We conduct statutory audits under the Companies Act, prepare the audit report and help you address audit observations.

We draft the Directors' Report covering all points required by the Act and help obtain Board approvals and signatures.

For the Annual General Meeting, we prepare and send the notice and agenda, help conduct the meeting as per statutory requirements and record the minutes.
