Indirect Tax

GST registration, return filing, refunds and advisory for your business.

Overview

Goods and Services Tax (GST) is a comprehensive, multi-stage, destination-based tax. Our GST advisory and compliance services help businesses follow GST law, manage their tax liability and avoid penalties.

Registration and return filing

We check whether your business needs GST registration and help you apply for a GSTIN.

After registration, we file your GST returns on time, including GSTR-1, GSTR-3B, GSTR-4, GSTR-7 and the annual return GSTR-9. We also reconcile GSTR-2A/2B with your purchase register so that you can claim the correct input tax credit (ITC).

Advisory, audit and refunds

We advise on input tax credit, assist with GST audits and assessments, and handle GST refund claims. We also review transactions for their GST impact and advise on classification of goods and services.

For larger businesses, we can set up an internal GST compliance process, carry out periodic reviews and train your staff.

E-way bills and disputes

We help you generate e-way bills and keep the required documents in order.

In case of a dispute, we represent you before the appellate authorities and tribunals.
